About TechSkills Toolbox

TechSkills Toolbox is a free set of everyday tools from TechSkills for text, images, development, calculations and privacy.

There is no account to create. Text and JSON are processed in your browser and are not uploaded or saved.

The QR tool uses one small open-source script (qrcode-generator), loaded from cdnjs. It runs in your browser and your input is not sent to it.
